# Pink Syngonium — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-12. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Provisionally Syngonium podophyllum; identity/photo still held for owner review. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock. This catalogue item is withheld from automatic recommendations pending owner identity/photo review.

- Light: Bright filtered light for variegated foliage.
- Water: Keep moist but well drained; avoid soggy roots.
- Care effort: Regular moisture checks.
- Pets: Harmful if eaten; sap can irritate.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
