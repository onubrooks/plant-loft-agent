# Green Syngonium — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-09. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Provisionally Syngonium podophyllum; cultivar unconfirmed. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light.
- Water: Keep moist but well drained; avoid soggy roots.
- Care effort: Regular moisture checks.
- Pets: Harmful if eaten; sap can irritate.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
