# Spider Plant — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-08. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Chlorophytum comosum. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Indirect light; avoid strong direct sun.
- Water: Keep moderately moist with drainage; avoid excessive feeding.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Listed non-toxic to cats/dogs; still discourage chewing.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
