# Golden Pothos — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-07. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Epipremnum aureum. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light; tolerates lower light.
- Water: Let the mix dry between waterings; drain well.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Harmful if chewed; keep away from pets.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
