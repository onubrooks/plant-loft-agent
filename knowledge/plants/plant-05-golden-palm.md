# Golden Palm — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-05. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Provisionally Dypsis lutescens / Chrysalidocarpus lutescens; confirm owner stock. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright filtered light.
- Water: Let the surface dry slightly; water and drain well.
- Care effort: Regular moisture checks.
- Pets: Named areca palm is listed non-toxic to cats/dogs; confirm identity.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.

Sources:
- https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/types/trees/indoor-palms
- https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/areca-palm
