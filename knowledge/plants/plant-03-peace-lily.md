# Peace Lily — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-03. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Spathiphyllum species. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Indirect light; avoid harsh direct sun.
- Water: Keep moderately moist, not saturated; check soil before watering.
- Care effort: Regular moisture checks.
- Pets: Toxic to cats and dogs; chewing can cause oral irritation, drooling, vomiting or difficulty swallowing.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
