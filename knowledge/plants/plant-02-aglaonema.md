# Aglaonema — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-02. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Aglaonema species; cultivar unconfirmed. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock. This catalogue item is withheld from automatic recommendations pending owner identity/photo review.

- Light: Indirect light; tolerates lower light.
- Water: Keep lightly moist, never waterlogged; allow surface drying.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Potentially harmful if chewed; keep away from pets.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
