# Philodendron — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-11. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Philodendron species; exact species and growth habit unconfirmed. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light; avoid scorching sun.
- Water: Keep well drained and moderately moist; avoid overwatering.
- Care effort: Ask whether climbing or self-heading.
- Pets: Potentially harmful if chewed; keep away from pets.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
