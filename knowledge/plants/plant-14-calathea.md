# Calathea — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-14. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Calathea trade group; may be Goeppertia, Ctenanthe or Stromanthe. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light.
- Water: Keep evenly moist with drainage; never saturated or fully dry.
- Care effort: Needs warm, humid conditions.
- Pets: Named Calathea is listed non-toxic to cats/dogs; confirm exact plant.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
