# Boston Fern — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-16. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Provisionally Nephrolepis exaltata; confirm Boston-fern identity. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light.
- Water: Keep the mix moist with drainage; do not let it dry completely.
- Care effort: Needs humid air; check moisture regularly.
- Pets: Named Boston fern is listed non-toxic to cats/dogs.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
