# Aloe Vera — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-17. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Aloe vera (syn. Aloe barbadensis). This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright light; suitable sun exposure after gradual adjustment.
- Water: Let the mix dry completely; use freely draining succulent mix.
- Care effort: Lower routine care; avoid overwatering.
- Pets: Toxic to cats and dogs; not edible advice.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
