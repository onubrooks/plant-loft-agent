# Monstera — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-10. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Monstera species; care baseline uses M. deliciosa, not confirmed identity. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light.
- Water: Keep well drained and moderately moist; avoid waterlogging.
- Care effort: Allow growing space and climbing support.
- Pets: Harmful if chewed; keep away from pets.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
