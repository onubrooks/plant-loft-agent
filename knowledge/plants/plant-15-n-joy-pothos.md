# N'Joy Pothos — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-15. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Epipremnum 'Njoy' / 'Hansoti12'; cultivar label requires confirmation. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright filtered light; shade may reduce variegation.
- Water: Keep well drained; allow drying between waterings.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Harmful if chewed; keep away from pets.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
