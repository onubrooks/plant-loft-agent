# Snake Plant — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-01. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Dracaena trifasciata (syn. Sansevieria trifasciata). This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright indirect light; tolerates lower light.
- Water: Allow the mix to dry before watering; drain completely.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Toxic to cats and dogs; prevent chewing.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
