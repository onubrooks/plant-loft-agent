# Lady Palm — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-06. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Provisionally Rhapis excelsa; confirm owner stock. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Indirect light; tolerates shade.
- Water: Water regularly with good drainage; avoid saturated roots.
- Care effort: Lower routine care.
- Pets: Named lady palm is listed non-toxic to cats/dogs; confirm identity.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
