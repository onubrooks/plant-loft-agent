# Schefflera — care and consultation

Catalogue ID: plant-04. Research checked: 25 October 2026.

Identity: Heptapleurum species (formerly Schefflera); exact species unconfirmed. This is guidance for the named plant group, not verification of the photographed stock.

- Light: Bright filtered light.
- Water: Let the mix dry before watering thoroughly; drain well.
- Care effort: Moderate routine care.
- Pets: Harmful to cats and dogs if chewed.

Use the shared care rules. Do not convert these conditions into a fixed watering calendar.
